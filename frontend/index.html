ESCALATION CATALOG

When automation hands off

Every stop condition maps to a clear next step.

09 RULES
ESC-00
Capture or policy pre-check failed

Expired policy, video below 720p or outside 5–60 seconds, poor lighting/sharpness, or editing metadata.

Re-record↺Ask for new captureGuide the claimant to daylight and a steady live walkaround.
ESC-01
Vehicle or plate does not match

Registration or vehicle colour differs from the insured policy record.

Identity mismatch!Block and escalateRoute to the fraud review queue; do not issue an offer.
ESC-02
Video continuity is broken

A sudden scene cut or loss of the tracked vehicle between plate and damage.

Cut / splice↺Require live captureAsk for one unbroken browser-camera recording.
ESC-03
Previously paid, unrepaired damage

The same canonical vehicle part has an open cash-paid damage record.

Repeat claim↗Pause and reviewRequest repair proof or send to an adjuster.
ESC-04
Signs of old rust or oxidation

Brown rust or flaking filler suggests pre-existing damage.

Pre-existing↗Escalate for reviewDo not treat uncertain age as a new incident.
ESC-05
Damage on opposite vehicle sides

Opposite-side impacts may indicate separate incidents bundled together.

Multiple hits↗Separate the incidentsAsk for clarification and adjuster review.
ESC-06
Safety-critical or hidden risk

Cracked windshield, damaged wheel/tyre, radiator impact, or panel-gap shift.

Safety concern!Physical inspectionRoute to a garage; no cash-in-lieu decision.
ESC-07
Confidence below 55%

Uncertain part/damage detection, or evidence may be dirt, glare, or a reflection.

Low confidence↗Human desk adjusterAsk for a clearer close-up if it can resolve the uncertainty.
ESC-08
Estimated net repair above ₹25,000

Claim exceeds the autonomous approval ceiling, regardless of customer offer.

Above cap!Licensed surveyorMove to human assessment; do not negotiate outside the limit.
